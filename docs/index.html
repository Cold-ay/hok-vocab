Hok Vocabulary

A world of words. Make it yours.

Getting started

  1. Create your first concept to group words by a topic.
  2. Add a word, its translation, and any details you want to remember. Online lookup is optional.
  3. Open a concept to practice your flashcards. Mark remembered words as learned and bookmark words to review.

Your words and progress

Your library and learning history are saved locally. No account is required. Keep the app’s data if you want to keep your words; removing the app or clearing its storage may remove the library. Device backups are managed by your operating system.

Online definitions and translations need an internet connection. If a lookup is unavailable, you can enter the information manually and continue practicing saved words.

Need help?

Report a problem or request a feature in the project’s support tracker. Include your app version, device model, and steps to reproduce the issue. Public issues are visible to others; keep private information out of your report.
