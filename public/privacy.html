Privacy & data

Hok Vocabulary helps you collect and practice words without an account.

Your library

Words, concepts, selected images, bookmarks, goals, learning activity, and preferences are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. Hok does not operate a cloud account or synchronization service. Device backups managed by your operating system may include app data.

Optional online lookups

When you choose Look up, the entered text is sent to DictionaryAPI (api.dictionaryapi.dev) and Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com). Changing your source language may send saved definitions and example text for translation. These providers also receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address. Their own terms and privacy practices apply. Manual entry and practice with saved words do not need these requests.

Images and permissions

Only images you choose with the system file picker are read. Images are stored locally and are not sent to the lookup services. The app has no advertising, analytics, location access, or tracking SDK configured.

Deleting data and support

Clear the app’s storage or remove the app to delete its local data. There is no cloud account to delete. For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact details in the store listing where you downloaded Hok Vocabulary.
