Price/business model: Free, no ads, no in-app purchases in this version.

Description:
Build a vocabulary that stays with you. Hok Vocabulary gives your words a home: organize them into concepts, practice with flashcards, and keep a steady learning habit.

• Create your own concepts and word collections.
• Add translations, pronunciation, definitions, examples, and images.
• Look up definitions and translations when online, or enter words manually.
• Bookmark words and review them at your own pace.
• Track daily practice, study time, streaks, and personal goals.
• Choose light or dark appearance and seven interface languages.

No account is needed. Your library is stored on your device. Optional online lookups send entered text to dictionary and translation providers. Device data is not synchronized to a cloud account.

Review notes:
