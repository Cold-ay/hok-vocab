Full description:
Make room for new words. Hok Vocabulary is a personal vocabulary trainer that helps you organize, remember, and practice the words that matter to you.

Create concepts and collect words with translations, definitions, pronunciation, example sentences, and your own images. Review your collection with flashcards, bookmark difficult words, and follow your progress with daily practice counts, study time, streaks, and goals.

Enjoy a responsive interface, light and dark themes, and seven interface languages. No account is needed. Words and learning history stay on your device. Optional dictionary and translation lookups use an internet connection; manual entry and saved-word practice are available offline.
